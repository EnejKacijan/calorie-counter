Privacy & support

Intake is operated by Enej Kacijan. For support, privacy questions or requests, contact enej.kacijan@gmail.com.

Your data on this device

Your profile, body measurements, nutrition goals, food and exercise diary, food library, saved foods and meals, preferences and assistant conversations are stored in this browser or app's local storage. There is no cloud synchronization or automatic backup.

Use Profile → Data & privacy to export a backup, restore one, or delete all local data. Backups contain personal health information; protect downloaded files. Deletion in Intake does not delete copies you have exported or data already transmitted to a service provider.

Optional online features

Optional food photos and Saved-food covers are stored separately as resized images in this device's IndexedDB storage. Retained copies are re-encoded without EXIF location metadata. They are not automatically shared with Assistant. Barcode and nutrition-label captures are not retained as food thumbnails.

In Data & privacy, “Include food photos” exports a local .intake backup package containing data and images. Turning it off exports text-only JSON without photo attachments. Clearing local data deletes retained photos too. Old JSON backups remain supported.

Dedicated barcode photos are decoded on your device; only the decoded number is sent for product lookup. Nutrition-label photos are sent to OpenAI only after permission to transcribe their printed values. Labels may be misread, so check the portion basis and all values before logging.

AI food photos, food descriptions and corrections, and assistant messages are processed through Intake's server by OpenAI. Before first use of each category, Intake asks permission. Assistant requests include conversation history and, when enabled, selected diary context, measurements and goals. Previous messages may themselves contain diary details. You can reset AI permissions in Profile, disable diary context, or use manual logging without AI.

Food searches and barcode lookups use USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts through the server. The relevant search terms or barcode are sent to those services. Network services receive connection metadata; a hosting provider may also keep access logs. Provider processing and retention must not be understood as zero-retention.

Adults and nutrition estimates

Intake is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Nutrition and exercise estimates are not medical advice or a diagnosis. Review AI estimates before saving them. Consult a qualified professional if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, have a medical condition or a history of eating disorders.
